# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee data using Microsoft Power BI to understand employee attrition and identify workforce groups with higher attrition rates.

An interactive dashboard was created to provide HR teams with a clear view of employee demographics, salary levels, job roles, and attrition patterns.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The organization was experiencing employee attrition and lacked clear visibility into which employee groups were more likely to leave.

The analysis aims to identify key attrition patterns and provide insights that can support better employee retention and workforce planning decisions.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze total employee count and attrition.
* Calculate the overall attrition rate.
* Analyze average employee age, salary, and years of experience.
* Identify attrition patterns by education.
* Analyze attrition across different age groups.
* Analyze attrition by salary slab.
* Identify job roles with higher attrition.
* Present insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Employee Overview

Analyzed the overall workforce and key HR metrics.

**Key Metrics:**

* Total Employees: **1,470**
* Total Attrition: **237**
* Attrition Rate: **16.1%**
* Average Age: **37 years**
* Average Salary: **6.5K**
* Average Years of Experience: **7 years**

### 2. Attrition by Education

Analyzed employee attrition across different educational backgrounds.

**Insight:**
Employees from the **Life Sciences** education category represented the highest share of attrition.

### 3. Attrition by Age

Analyzed attrition across different employee age groups.

**Insight:**
Employees in the **26–35 age group** showed the highest attrition.

### 4. Attrition by Salary

Analyzed employee attrition across different salary slabs.

**Insight:**
Employees earning **up to 5K** recorded the highest number of attritions.

### 5. Attrition by Job Role

Compared attrition across different job roles.

**Insight:**
**Laboratory Technicians** recorded the highest attrition among the analyzed job roles.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* The organization had **1,470 employees** and **237 attritions**.
* The overall attrition rate was **16.1%**.
* The **26–35 age group** represented a key attrition segment.
* Employees earning up to **5K** had the highest number of attritions.
* Life Sciences had the highest share of attrition by education.
* Laboratory Technicians recorded the highest attrition among job roles.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, HR could consider:

* Developing targeted retention strategies for employees aged 26–35.
* Reviewing compensation and career-growth opportunities for lower salary groups.
* Investigating workload, job satisfaction, and career progression for high-attrition roles.
* Conducting further analysis to understand the reasons behind employee exits.
* Monitoring attrition trends regularly through an HR dashboard.

## 📌 Result

The dashboard helped identify **overall employee attrition patterns, high-risk age groups, salary levels, education backgrounds, and job roles**. These insights can support HR teams in developing more targeted **employee retention strategies, workforce planning, and data-driven HR decisions**.
